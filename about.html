About PSR

Experience, partnerships and vision — built around responsible action.

PSR was formed with a clear purpose: to address the challenges corporates and NGOs face in finding a trusted partner capable of delivering compliance-driven CSR projects that create lasting, measurable impact.

PSREst. purpose-first
Who we are

A multidisciplinary partner for CSR teams.

Our team brings together expertise in law, corporate governance, financial operations, banking, technology and NGO regulatory compliance. This lets PSR design and deliver projects that meet high standards of CSR accountability — not just on paper, but in the field.

Our strength lies in creating transparent, auditable and scalable models aligned with Schedule VII CSR mandates and the individual goals of corporates, foundations and philanthropic organisations.

We do not chase scale for its own sake. We take on engagements where a structured, honest view of the work — the risks, the trade-offs and the outcomes — is genuinely wanted.

What we stand for

Three principles guide the work.

01

Transparency

Clear reporting and accountable processes that help stakeholders understand exactly how CSR resources are being used, at every stage.

02

Measurable impact

A focus on outcomes, evidence and assessment rather than activity counts alone. If it can't be observed, it can't be improved.

03

Long-term value

Models designed to be practical and sustainable — aligned with the goals of both communities and corporate stakeholders across cycles.

Vision

Community development in complete harmony with corporate responsibility.

Mission

Support corporates in achieving CSR goals as per their CSR Policy; promote transparency, accountability and measurable impact in CSR spending; and abridge the trust and compliance gap between corporates and not-for-profit organisations.
