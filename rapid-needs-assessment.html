Understand the need before you spend.

A quick, structured needs assessment after an event or within a priority geography — using desk research, secondary data and primary field work to inform where CSR should flow.

Structured · evidence-led · decision-ready
Why this matters

Many CSR projects begin with a solution and then look for a problem to attach it to. A rapid needs assessment inverts that — grounding funding decisions in what communities and local systems actually require.

What this can cover
  • Secondary data & desk research
  • Rapid primary field interactions
  • Stakeholder mapping
  • Thematic & geographic prioritisation
  • Alignment with Schedule VII themes
  • Actionable investment options
How we work

A clear sequence from question to decision.

01

Objective & geography

Agree what decision the assessment must inform — geography, theme, timeline.

02

Desk phase

Rapid synthesis of demographic, health, education and livelihoods data for the geography.

03

Stakeholder mapping

Identify government, NGO, community and corporate actors already active in the space.

04

Field phase

Short, structured field visits — key informants, community groups and partner interactions.

05

Options framing

Translate needs into a small set of investable options with clear trade-offs.

06

Decision brief

A brief the CSR committee can act on — not a 200-page report that nobody reads.

Questions we help answer

What would a decision-maker need to know?

  • What does the community actually need?
  • Who is already working on this?
  • What is the smallest sensible first step?
  • Which options are the best fit for our CSR policy?
