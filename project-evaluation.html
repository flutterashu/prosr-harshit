Why this matters

Evaluations are often written to justify what was done. PSR's evaluation is written to inform what happens next — with the same rigour whether the findings are flattering or not.

What this can cover
  • Outcome & impact evaluation
  • Deviation analysis vs. intended impact
  • Beneficiary voice & field verification
  • Recommendations for the next cycle
  • Learning documentation
  • Board- and disclosure-ready summaries
How we work

A clear sequence from question to decision.

01

Scope & evaluation framework

Agree the questions the evaluation must answer and the framework — outcomes, OECD-DAC lens or custom.

02

Baseline reconstruction

Where a baseline is missing, reconstruct it from records, interviews and secondary data.

03

Field & stakeholder work

Structured interactions with beneficiaries, partner staff and local stakeholders.

04

Analysis

Cross-source triangulation, deviation analysis and outcome mapping.

05

Draft & validation

Draft findings, walk the partner through them, and validate before finalising.

06

Committee-ready reporting

A report that speaks to the CSR committee, the corporate donor and, where relevant, public disclosure.

Questions we help answer

What would a decision-maker need to know?

  • Did the project deliver the intended outcomes?
  • Where did results deviate — and why?
  • What worked well enough to scale or repeat?
  • What should the next cycle stop doing?
