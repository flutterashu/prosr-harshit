Structured · evidence-led · decision-ready
Why this matters

For corporate CSR teams, execution risk sits between approval and outcome. Our project management support makes responsibilities, progress, evidence and reporting visible — so the committee sees the real project, not a curated version of it.

What this can cover
  • Project planning & design
  • Selection of implementing partners
  • Define policy & CSR committee charter
  • Review of existing CSR activities
  • Project implementation review
  • Structured periodic reporting
How we work

A clear sequence from question to decision.

01

Project planning & design

Translate the CSR intent into a designed project with objectives, indicators, geography, budget logic and clear roles.

02

Partner assessment

Structured due diligence of implementing partners — governance, financial health, field capability and track record.

03

Policy & committee support

Draft or refine the CSR policy, committee terms and decision templates aligned with Schedule VII.

04

Portfolio review

Review existing CSR activities to surface gaps, overlaps and reallocation opportunities.

05

Implementation review

Structured review of live projects — evidence collection, site visits where relevant, and issue escalation.

06

Committee-ready reporting

Reports designed for CSR committees, boards and disclosures — not just internal circulation.

Questions we help answer

What would a decision-maker need to know?

  • Is the project designed around an identified need?
  • Is the implementing partner appropriate and sufficiently assessed?
  • Are implementation and reporting mechanisms clear?
  • Are emerging issues reaching decision-makers in time?
