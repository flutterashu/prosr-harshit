Our approach

A connected operating view of responsible investment.

PSR combines planning, partner selection, governance, monitoring, assessment and reporting — so CSR stakeholders move from intent to informed action, and stay there through the full cycle.

The PSR lifecycle

Six stages. One accountable story.

Understand

Start with the objective, geography, community need, stakeholder context and compliance requirements — before any solution is proposed.

01
02

Plan

Translate the objective into a structured project design — proposal, indicators, implementation logic and a clear theory of change.

Partner

Assess and select implementing partners with the right field capability and governance — and set up the review mechanisms that will hold.

03
04

Monitor

Use reports, reviews and site visits where relevant to keep visibility during implementation — and to surface emerging issues in time.

Assess

Measure baseline, progress, outcomes and impact — at the stage and depth appropriate to the project and the decision it must inform.

05
06

Decide

Turn findings into reporting, recommendations and better-informed future CSR decisions — closing the loop back to Understand.

Governance lens

Compliance is part of the project design — not an afterthought.

PSR's expertise spans law, corporate governance, financial operations and NGO regulatory compliance. That multidisciplinary foundation informs how we approach every CSR project and partnership.

Our support areas include fund management, risk analysis, partnerships, governance & monitoring, reporting and decision making. Governance is designed into the operating rhythm from day one — so audit trails, committee cadences and disclosures are natural outputs, not year-end scrambles.
